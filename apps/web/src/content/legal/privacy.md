This Privacy Policy explains how Fortuna ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses,
stores and shares personal data when you use the Fortuna application. We are
the data controller for the personal data described below and we process it in
accordance with the Brazilian General Data Protection Law (Lei nº 13.709/2018,
"LGPD").

## Information We Collect

When you sign in to Fortuna with Google, we receive and store the following
profile information from your Google Account:

- **Name** — the display name on your Google profile.
- **Email address** — the primary email address associated with your Google
  Account.
- **Profile picture URL** — a link to the avatar image on your Google profile.
- **Google account identifier** — an opaque, Google-issued identifier that we
  use to recognise you on return visits.

In addition, we generate and store the following data when you use Fortuna:

- **Session records** — an opaque, per-device session identifier and metadata
  such as the device user-agent and the timestamp of last activity.
- **Application activity** — records of the financial entries, categories and
  preferences you create inside Fortuna.

We do **not** collect financial credentials (such as banking passwords),
payment card numbers, or location data.

## How We Use Your Information

We process the personal data described above for the following purposes:

- **Authentication** — verifying your identity through Google Sign-In so you
  can access your Fortuna account.
- **Session management** — keeping you signed in across devices and revoking
  sessions when you sign out or delete your account.
- **Providing the service** — storing and displaying the financial data you
  enter so you can manage your finances.
- **Security and abuse prevention** — detecting suspicious sign-in activity
  and protecting accounts from unauthorised access.

## Legal Basis (LGPD)

Under the LGPD, we rely on the following legal bases to process your data:

- **Consent (Art. 7, I)** — for storing your Google profile information and
  for processing the financial data you choose to enter into Fortuna.
- **Execution of a contract (Art. 7, V)** — for authentication, session
  management and the core delivery of the service you have signed up for.
- **Legitimate interests (Art. 7, IX)** — for security, abuse prevention and
  service-integrity purposes.

## Data Retention

We retain your personal data for as long as your Fortuna account is active.
When you delete your account through the in-product deletion flow, we
permanently erase your profile information, active sessions and application
activity from our primary data store. Backups containing personal data are
retained for no longer than thirty (30) days, after which they are overwritten
in the normal course of our backup rotation.

## Your Rights

The LGPD grants you the following rights with respect to your personal data:

- **Confirmation and access** — to confirm that we process your data and to
  obtain a copy of it.
- **Correction** — to ask us to correct incomplete, inaccurate or out-of-date
  data.
- **Anonymisation, blocking or deletion** — of data that is unnecessary,
  excessive or processed in violation of the LGPD.
- **Portability** — to request your data in a structured, commonly used
  format.
- **Deletion of data processed with consent** — to request erasure of data we
  hold on the basis of your consent.
- **Information about sharing** — to learn which public and private entities
  we have shared your data with.
- **Information about the consequences** of refusing to provide consent.
- **Revocation of consent** — to withdraw consent you have previously given.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details in the
"Contact" section below.

## Account Deletion

You can delete your Fortuna account at any time from the in-product account
settings. Deletion is self-service, permanent and irreversible: it removes
your profile information, your active sessions, and the financial data you
have entered into Fortuna. Backups containing your data are overwritten on
the schedule described in "Data Retention".

## Sharing Your Information

We do not sell your personal data. We share data only with the following
categories of recipient and only to the extent necessary:

- **Google LLC** — for the Google Sign-In flow described above.
- **Infrastructure providers** — cloud hosting and database providers that
  store and process data on our behalf under contractual data-protection
  obligations.

## Children's Privacy

Fortuna is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not
knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe that a child
has provided us with personal data, please contact us so we can delete it.

## International Transfers

Your personal data may be processed and stored on servers located outside
Brazil. When that happens, we rely on the safeguards permitted by the LGPD
(including Art. 33) to ensure your data remains protected.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will
update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to
review this page periodically.
